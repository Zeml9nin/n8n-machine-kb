Double Opt-In Funnel Without a Mailing Tool

2026-11-02 · digest

Collect subscribers legally with three small workflows.

1. Building Blocks
• Sign-up: A Webhook stores the address as pending and mails a signed confirmation link.
• Signature: Sign the token with the Crypto node (HMAC) and add an expiry time.

2. Confirm and Leave
• The confirm webhook verifies signature and expiry, then marks the contact confirmed.
• Put an unsubscribe link in every mail and handle it with its own webhook.
• Never mail a contact whose status is not confirmed.
