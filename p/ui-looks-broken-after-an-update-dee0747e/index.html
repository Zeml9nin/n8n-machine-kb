UI Looks Broken After an Update

2026-11-02 · digest

Greyed-out or half-loaded screens right after upgrading are often a stale browser or proxy cache.

1. Symptom and Cause
• Symptom: After the server updates, the editor shows odd colors or missing parts.
• Why: Old front-end files stay cached in the browser or in a reverse proxy.

2. Checks
• Hard-reload the page and try a private window.
• Purge the static-asset cache of your proxy or CDN.
• Confirm the running version in Settings before changing anything else.
