Sub-Workflows: Solving "Workflow is not active"

2026-11-02 · digest

Build modular workflows without activation errors.

1. Symptom and cause
• Execute Sub-workflow throws Workflow is not active and cannot be executed or returns empty data.
• The child starts with a Manual or Webhook trigger instead of the dedicated one.

2. Fix
• Child: start with When Executed by Another Workflow; it may stay inactive.
• Parent: use Execute Sub-workflow and enable Wait for Sub-Workflow Completion.
