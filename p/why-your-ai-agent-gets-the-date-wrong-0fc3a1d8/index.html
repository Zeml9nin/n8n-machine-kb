Why Your AI Agent Gets the Date Wrong

2026-11-02 · digest

Models do not know today, and calendar tools expose it quickly.

1. Symptom and Cause
• Symptom: The agent books meetings in the past or says the wrong weekday.
• Why: The model has a training cutoff and no clock.

2. The Fix
• Write the current date and timezone into the system prompt with an expression that uses $now.
• Or add the Date & Time tool so the agent can ask for it.
• Return dates in ISO 8601 form between nodes.
