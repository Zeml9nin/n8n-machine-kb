Original

Posting model output to a channel needs limits, or one bad prompt floods it.

01Symptom and Cause

  • Symptom: A loop posts dozens of long messages and Slack rate-limits the bot.
  • Why: One message per item, with no cap on length or count.

02Fix

  • Join items into one digest message before the Slack node.
  • Cut the text to a fixed length and keep the full answer in a log.
  • Use a dedicated bot credential with access to one channel only.
